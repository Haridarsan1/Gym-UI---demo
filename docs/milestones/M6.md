## Accessibility

- **axe** (`@axe-core/playwright` 4.14.0) runs on every screen in `e2e/responsive.spec.ts` at 390 and 1440 px (member, trainer, desk, finance, owner, branch manager and admin screens). Any serious or critical violation fails the test, with the rule, the elements and the fix. Rules: WCAG 2 A and AA, WCAG 2.1 A and AA, and axe’s best practices.
- **Found and fixed:** low-contrast “subtle” text (`clay-subtle` darkened to 4.7:1) and white-on-orange buttons (a new `ember-650`, 4.7:1, for buttons with white text; the marketing orange is unchanged); accessible names that did not contain the words shown (logo link, command palette button, sidebar items, “Remind”, “Accept with a note”, “Mark paid”, the exercise picker, “Back”, invoice links); a definition list with paragraphs in it; a scrolling step list that a keyboard could not reach; a phone card button that wrapped links.
- **Names follow what is shown** (WCAG 2.5.3): a link that shows a short word (“Start workout”, “Profile and settings”) is named by it, and the full wording is its tooltip.
- **Manual:** `docs/a11y-manual-check.md`, a 15-minute VoiceOver checklist for iPhone and Mac (login, log a set, book, desk check-in, pay an invoice). I cannot run a screen reader, so this is for a person.

## Demo guide and reset

- `/demo` (`pages/DemoGuidePage.tsx`, scenarios in `app/demoScenarios.ts`): six scenarios, each with one line on what it shows, who you are, and a **Start** button that signs in as that person (`signInDemo`) and opens the screen with a full load. Starting resets nothing (`e2e/demo-guide.spec.ts` checks the seed time is unchanged). Linked from the login page (“Try the demo as”) and from the account menu (demo mode only).
- The scenarios: log a workout as Arjun (`/m`), review a client as Coach Meera (`/t/clients/mem_arjun/review`), check in a member at the front desk (`/desk`), approve a refund as finance (`/desk/finance/refunds`), see the dashboard as the owner (`/owner`), support access as Ember admin (`/admin/support`).
- **Reset demo data** is a separate action: in the account menu and at the bottom of the guide, each asking first (“Keep my changes” is the focused choice). The account menu’s confirmation lives in `AvatarMenu`, not in the panel, because the panel closes the moment it is used.
- Fixed on the way: on phones the account menu ran under the bottom tab bar and hid Sign out; it is now capped above the bar.
