# M3 · Front desk and finance (R03)

Detailed build notes. The checklist is in [docs/PLAN.md](../PLAN.md#7-milestones); the product requirements are in [docs/REQUIREMENTS.md](../REQUIREMENTS.md).

Console style: calm and fast, dense tables that become card lists on phones, ⌘K, keyboard shortcuts for frequent desk actions, a sticky action bar. No confetti, mascots or badges. Checkpoint 1 follows step 2; Checkpoint 2 follows finance.
- [x] Step 0: foundations
  - **GST document numbers** (`domain/numbering.ts`, `api/numbering.ts`, `NumberSeries`, tested): consecutive and unique within each Indian financial year (April to March) for each series, at most 16 characters. An invoice is the branch’s prefix, the year and four digits, for example `PFAND/26-27/0001` (`Branch.invoicePrefix`, 3 to 5 capital letters or digits: PFAND, PFBAN, PFPOW); a credit note has its own series (`CNAND/26-27/0001`). The number is taken from the series counter inside the transaction that stores the document, so concurrent documents get different numbers, a failed transaction gives its number back (no gaps), and a void invoice keeps its number. The seed numbers every invoice in issue order per branch and year (seed v12, Dexie v7), checkout allocates through it, and the receipt page shows it. Your example `PF-AND/26-27/0001` is 17 characters, so the prefix has no hyphen.
  - **Desk API door** (`api/desk.ts`): `openDesk` (desk, finance, branch managers and owners only; an action check; the branches they may act in), `assertInBranch`, `memberInScope` (a member in another branch is “not found”).
  - **No health access, anywhere** (`api/deskRestrictions.test.ts`): every health and training action is refused to front desk and finance for any record, no desk screen is behind or named for one, the sensitive door and the trainer and member APIs refuse them (and the owner and branch manager, who have no switch on here), and nothing is audited for a refused view.
  - **Records and policies:** `Booking.roomId` (optional; conflict-checked only when set), `GymPolicies.partialPaymentsEnabled` (off).
  - **Bundle guard** also for `/desk`: no member, trainer, owner or admin code, and no QR scanner, in its first download (budget 170 KB; 149.6 KB now).
  - **Hooks:** `.githooks/pre-commit` (the docs check) and `.githooks/pre-push` (typecheck and unit tests; a failure refuses the push), installed by `npm install`.

Both load only when “Scan QR” is opened; the bundle report fails if they reach the desk’s first download.
  - **Rules** are the member pass’s (`domain/checkin.ts`, `checkInDecision`, 10-minute scan window), plus `staffDenyText` for what the desk reads. The answer is worked out from local data; a test asserts it is on screen in under a second.
  - **API** (`api/checkin.ts`, Zod-checked, lazy; `api/deskHooks.ts`): `searchMembers` (name, phone or pass code, two characters or more, the desk’s own branches only), `checkInMember` (by member or by scanned token; every attempt is recorded, a denial too; a visit pass loses a visit on entry; one key makes one check-in), `overrideCheckIn` (needs the person’s own `canOverrideCheckIn`, a reason, and a denial to override; a new check-in beside the denial, no visit used, audited `checkin.override`, one visit only: the next scan is judged by the rules again), `getTodayCheckIns` (live, refreshed every 5 seconds). Finance has no door here, and the desk can’t reach another branch.
  - **Screen** (`features/desk/CheckIn.tsx`, the desk home): search, the large result and today’s list, which sits beside them from 1280 px and under them below. The result is one icon, one line and the member’s name in type 34 to 48 px, with the plan, valid to, visits left, what is owed and the branch; the six states are allowed, allowed with dues (the amount), grace (“Checked in, membership ended”), denied (why, in staff words, and “Let in anyway…” for those who may), repeat scan (“Already checked in at 5:42 pm”) and override. Colour is never the only signal. No confetti. Keys: `/` search, `S` scan, `Enter` check in the highlighted member, `N` next member.
  - **Scanner** (`features/desk/scan/Scanner.tsx`, lazy): the camera, the browser’s detector or ZXing, and a typed pass code when there is no camera or it is refused. Tested with a fake camera.
- [x] Step 2: New member wizard (T01, M01, M03, O03). **Checkpoint 1** (`docs/checkpoints/m3-1/{390,768,1440}/`, `npm run m3:shots1`)
  - **API** (`api/newMember.ts`, Zod-checked, lazy): `getWizardOptions` (plans on sale at the branch with totals, the branch’s trainers, partial payments, the person’s own discount limit), `checkDuplicates` and `registerMember`. A phone number or an email that already belongs to a member is **flagged, not refused** (families share numbers, and parents register children on their own): the desk sees who has it and chooses “Same person: open their profile” or “Different person sharing this number”, which continues and is recorded on the new member (`Member.sharesContactWith`, and in the audit event); an answer must cover everyone who has the number. The same name (across every branch) is still a warning the desk must answer (“This is a different person”). Consent: using their details is required; photos, assessments and offers are choices, each recorded with the privacy notice version and who recorded it.
  - **One transaction behind one key:** the member, consents, trainer assignment (with its effective date), the membership with the plan’s terms copied in (a later price change never touches it), the GST invoice (number from the branch’s series, a discount within the person’s own limit, taken off before tax), the first payment (cash, UPI, card or transfer; the full amount unless the gym allows part payments) with its receipt number, training credits when paid, an audit event (`membership.create`), and the welcome with the pass code and the receipt in the email log. A double press, a retry or two at once make one member. Everything that can be wrong is said before anything is written. A membership starts when it is paid in full (scheduled if its start date is ahead); pay later leaves it pending and the invoice open, and the pass says so at the door; a free trial needs no invoice.
  - **Screen** (`features/desk/NewMember.tsx`, `/desk/new`): Details, Consent, Plan, Trainer, Payment, then the member’s pass (QR, code, printable; emailed, or “print it” when there is no email). The duplicate check runs as the phone number is typed. The draft is kept in the tab, so a reload loses nothing. Phone: one column with Back and Next above the navigation bar. From 1280 px a summary sits beside the steps.
- [x] Step 3: Member profile and lifecycle (M02, M03, A02)
  - **Rules** (`domain/lifecycle.ts`, tested; the numbers are `GymPolicies`: `freezeMinDays` 7, `freezeMaxDays` 30, `maxFreezesPerMembership` 2, `cancellationFeePaise` 50,000): `effectiveStatus` (a freeze whose last day has passed has ended, and a cancellation whose date has come has taken effect: check-in follows this, not the stored status), freeze from today to a date (the end date moves back by the days frozen) and early unfreeze (the unused days come back), `unusedCredit` and `quoteChange` (proration), `refundEligibility` and `cancelProblem`.
  - **Proration and the GST credit note:** a dearer time-based plan is an upgrade now: the new plan is invoiced in full, the unused days of the old plan pro rata on the **pre-tax price actually paid** come back as a **credit note** (its own number series, `CNAND/26-27/0001`, GST on the credit, linked to the original invoice), applied to the new invoice as a payment of method `credit_note` (so it is a document of its own, never netted silently, and reports count it apart from cash). The difference is collected like any invoice; paying it starts the new plan today and ends the old one the day before. A cheaper plan, a visit pass, or a credit that would cover the new plan is **not refunded or credited in cash**: it is invoiced to start when this period ends. Treatment “to be confirmed with the gym’s accountant” (Decisions).
  - **Cancel:** with a reason and an effective date (today, or later while it runs). Access continues until the date. Nothing is refunded automatically: the sheet shows what the member may be eligible for under policy (unused days pro rata on what was paid, less the cancellation fee, as a ceiling) with “Request refund”, which puts a `RefundRequest` (amount, reason, member, invoice) in the finance queue (step 4). The desk can’t approve refunds; asking is audited (`refund.request`). A cancellation can be taken back before its date.
  - **API:** `api/lifecycle.ts` (`freezeMembership`, `unfreezeMembership`, `getPlanChoices`, `changePlan`, `previewCancel`, `cancelMembership`, `keepMembership`, `requestRefund`), `api/payments.ts` (`collectPayment` and `applyPaid`: what a paid invoice does, once: starts the first membership and its training credits, an upgrade or a renewal, or adds sessions), `api/memberProfile.ts` (one member; every action says why it can’t be used; no health information, proved by a test). Each write is one transaction, audited, and idempotent by key.
  - **Screens:** `/desk/members` (`Members.tsx`: search, status filter, a dense table that is a card list on phones) and `/desk/members/:memberId` (`MemberProfile.tsx`, tabs in the address: Membership, Invoices and payments, Training credits, Bookings and check-ins, Trainer, Messages). Freeze, unfreeze, change plan, cancel, keep, collect payment, request refund and change trainer are sheets; finance sees the profile without the buttons it has no permission for. The trainer transfer screen (A02) is the Trainer tab with “Change trainer” (reason, date; the history shows each trainer with who assigned them and why).
- [x] Step 4: Money at the desk (M03, O04, M04, M06)
  - **Collect payment** (`api/payments.ts`, `/desk/payments/new` and the member’s Collect sheet): cash, UPI, card or bank transfer against an open invoice, in full or (when `partialPaymentsEnabled`) in part; more than the amount due is refused; one key makes one payment, even two at once; a receipt number (`RCP-2026-0001`) and a receipt email. A payment that settles an invoice does what it bought, once (`applyPaid`): the first membership and its training credits, a renewal or an upgrade (the old plan ends the day before), or a sessions pack. Finance can collect at any branch; the desk only its own.
  - **Receipts:** the member’s tax receipt is now `api/receiptData.ts` + `features/shared/ReceiptSheet.tsx`, so the desk shows and prints the same document (`/desk/members/:memberId/invoices/:invoiceId`, “View or print the receipt”).
  - **Dues and reminders** (`api/dues.ts`, `/desk/dues`): unpaid and part-paid invoices at the person’s branches, longest overdue first, with the totals, “Remind” (an email logged as `payment_reminder`, never twice in three days, skipped and logged when there is no email), “Collect” (opens the member’s sheet), and the last 14 days of declined online payments. The seed (v16) now has a dozen open and part-paid invoices, a few declines, and refund requests to work on.
  - **Refunds** (`api/refunds.ts`, `/desk/finance/refunds`): the finance queue of requests the desk made, each with what approving would do (which payment the money goes back against, the unused training credits taken back), and what was decided. Finance, a branch manager or the owner approves or rejects, both audited (`refund.request`, `refund.approve`, `refund.create`, `refund.reject`). Approving pays back against a real payment, never more than it still holds, within the person’s own `refundLimitPaise` (finance ₹25,000, a branch manager ₹50,000, the owner none: above it, “Ask the owner”), writes a `Refund`, reverses the unused credits the refunded money bought in proportion as a ledger `reversal`, and emails the member. A rejection needs a note the desk reads. `issueRefund` lets finance refund without a request, with the same checks. Money moves only on approval.
  - **Training credits (M06):** the Credits tab shows the balance (available, held, used) and the ledger with expiry. `api/creditsAdjust.ts` (`adjustCredits`: add or remove with a reason, ledger `adjustment` and audit, never below zero; owner and branch manager) is tested; its screen comes with the owner console in M4, because the owner and branch managers work in `/owner`, not `/desk`.
- [x] Step 5: Bookings calendar by trainer and room, conflict-safe (A03), with the desk side of trainer leave conflicts
  - **API** (`api/deskBookings.ts`): `getCalendar` (a day or a week at one branch: each trainer’s free times, leave, bookings with member, status and room, and the sessions that clash with leave), `getBookingFacts`, `bookForMember` (member in scope, active membership and a credit, trainer at the branch, the time still free, the member’s own diary, an optional room not in use; reserves one credit, emails the member, audited as `booking.move` with the reason “Booked at the front desk”; the unique slot index is the last defence, so two desks booking one time give one booking and the other gets “That time was just taken.” with the next free times), `getDeskConflictOptions` and `moveDeskConflict` (shared with the trainer’s move in `api/schedule.ts`: credit stays, member emailed, audited). `Booking.roomId` is set on seeded sessions (seed v17).
  - **Screen** `/desk/bookings` (`Bookings.tsx`, `bookings/BookSheet.tsx`, `bookings/MoveConflict.tsx`): day or week, by trainer or by room, in the address. From 768 px the day is a grid of times by trainer or room; on phones and for the week it is one trainer or room at a time as lists. Free times are “Book” buttons; a conflict banner lists sessions on leave with “Move”.
- [x] Step 6: Finance: invoices and payments, refund requests and approval (M04), reconciliation and flagged duplicate events, role-scoped CSV exports, day close for cash. **Checkpoint 2** (sheets in `docs/checkpoints/m3-2/`, `npm run m3:shots2`)
  - **Records** (Dexie v9, seed v18): `GatewaySettlement` (a gateway payout: its charges by reference, and its fee) and `DayClose` (one per branch and day); a gateway event gets `reviewedBy`/`reviewNote` and a payment’s reconciliation `reviewedBy`. The seed has an event for every online payment (one delivered three times), monthly payouts, and three mismatches: a payout that paid a different amount, a charge no payout has after three days, and a payout line Ember has no payment for.
  - **Invoices and payments** (`api/finance.ts`, `/desk/finance/invoices`): search by number, name, phone, receipt or reference; filter by status, branch and dates; invoices or payments. Billed, collected (money received, credit notes excluded), still due and refunded are shown apart and follow the filters.
  - **Reconciliation** (`domain/reconciliation.ts`, `/desk/finance/reconciliation`): online payments matched to payouts by gateway reference and amount; a different amount, or no payout after `SETTLEMENT_WINDOW_DAYS` (3), is flagged; a payout line with no payment, and a gateway event delivered more than once (it made one payment), are listed. “Accept with a note” records who and why (audited as `record.edit`) and the item stays accepted when matching runs again. The screen also lists the last cash day closes with any variance.
  - **Exports** (`createExport`, `/desk/finance/exports`): CSV of invoices, payments, refunds, GST credit notes or dues for the branches the person can see and a date range; names, ids and money only, no phone, email or health data; formula-like text is defused; every download writes `export.create`.
  - **Day close** (`api/dayClose.ts`, `domain/dayClose.ts`, `/desk/finance/day-close`, permission `cash.close`: desk, finance, branch manager, owner): expected cash (cash received less cash refunded, India time) against the count; a variance needs a note; closing locks the day: cash payments and cash refunds are refused on a closed day (`assertDayOpen`), card and UPI are not. `reopenDay` (`cash.reopen`, owner only, reason required, audited) is tested; its button comes with the owner console in M4, because the owner works in `/owner`, not `/desk`.
