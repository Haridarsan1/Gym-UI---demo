# M2 · Trainer app (R04)

Detailed build notes. The checklist is in [docs/PLAN.md](../PLAN.md#7-milestones); the product requirements are in [docs/REQUIREMENTS.md](../REQUIREMENTS.md).

Every trainer screen is responsive from the start: single column on phones, list and detail from 768 px, three zones at 1440 px (see Responsive patterns).
- [x] Foundations (step 1): trainer rosters in the seed, the rules behind Today, the trainer API, the `ThreeZone` layout, trainer e2e coverage
- [x] Today dashboard (T07)
  - **Seed v8** (`demo/seedTrainer.ts`): every member who has a trainer and an active membership gets a place in that trainer’s book. 25% are `strong` (a plan, nearly every workout done, a recent review), 25% `steady`, 30% `slipping` (fine until the last nine days, an overdue review) and 20% `awaiting` (no plan). Their logs are coach-led (the trainer entered them, T05) because they have no login. Meera has four sessions today (one done), every other trainer two, each holding a credit; about one client in six holds a pack of credits that ends within two weeks.
  - **Rules** (`domain/trainerToday.ts`, tested; the numbers are `GymPolicies`, edited by the owner in M4, with these defaults): a client is reviewed on the cadence their goals set (T02: the shortest `reviewEveryDays` among their active goals), and on `reviewCadenceDays` (28) only when no goal sets one. A review is due that many days after the newest shared review, or on its follow-up date when it has one, or that many days after the plan began when there has been none; private notes and the member’s check-ins are not reviews. A client has “missed” workouts when at least `missedWorkoutsThreshold` (2) scheduled days in the last `missedWorkoutsWindowDays` (7, not counting today) have nothing logged; skipped workouts and frozen days don’t count. Credits are “expiring” when unused ones sit in a pack that ends within `creditExpiryWarningDays` (14). “Waiting for a plan” is an active client with no published plan. Frozen clients are only ever listed for expiring credits. Seed v9 gives each roster client one goal with a two, four or six week cadence.
  - **API** (`api/trainer.ts`, loaded on demand): `getTrainerToday` returns only the caller’s own clients (their open assignment), for trainers only.
  - **Screen** (`features/trainer/Today.tsx`): phone, one column with the five lists stacked (three clients each and “Show all”); from 768 px the five lists are a menu on the left and the chosen list is beside it (`?show=`); from 1280 px a third zone shows the selected client at a glance (`?client=`), the next session’s client by default. Below 1280 px a row opens the client’s page instead, so the third zone never holds the only way to do anything.
- [x] Clients and client profile with authorship (step 2)
  - **Shared book** (`api/book.ts`): every client the trainer has today with all the signals worked out once, so Today, the list and the profile agree. `api/clients.ts`: `listClients` (search by name or phone, filter by flag: review overdue, behind, needs a plan, credits expiring, frozen), `getClient` (membership and credits, program versions with authors, four-week adherence and a weekly chart, recent logs with who entered each, goals with progress, shared reviews and the trainer’s own private notes, previous trainers), `addPrivateNote`. A client who is not the caller’s (or has moved away) is “not found”, so existence is not revealed.
  - **Trainer transfer (A02)** (`api/assignments.ts`, `trainer.assign`: owner, branch manager, front desk): the old assignment closes the day before, a new one opens, the change is audited with its reason. Plans, logs and shared reviews keep their author and are untouched; the new trainer sees them with authors; a private note stays with its author and the new trainer never sees it; the old trainer can no longer open the client. Tested end to end. The screen that triggers it is the member profile in M3.
  - **Screen** (`features/trainer/Clients.tsx`, one screen on `/t/clients` and `/t/clients/:memberId`; search, filter and tab are in the address): phone, the list then the profile with a Back link that returns to the same list; from 768 px list and profile side by side; from 1280 px a third zone with the client at a glance (membership, what needs attention, trainers), which on narrower screens is the top of the Overview tab. Tabs: Overview, Workouts (each says who entered it, “coach” for the trainer), Notes and reviews (add a private note).

  - **Consent is enforced at the one door** (`api/sensitive.ts`): without an active assessment consent, `viewSensitive` refuses staff (trainer included) with “Assessment consent withdrawn” (or “hasn’t agreed”), writes no audit view, and `saveAssessment` refuses to record. The member can still read their own. Withdrawing and agreeing again both take effect at once (the newest consent record decides). The records are kept.
  - **API** (`api/assessments.ts`, Zod-checked, lazy): `getAssessment` (state none, recorded, consent withdrawn or no consent; each staff view audited as `assessment.view`, repeat views included; goals and where each metric stands now), `saveAssessment` (audited as `record.edit`), `createGoal`, `reviseGoal` (the old goal is kept as Revised with the reason and a new active one starts with a history that says where it came from), `closeGoal` (a reason), `confirmGoalAchieved` (only the trainer; awards the Goal achieved badge once; repeating changes nothing). Each goal has its own `reviewEveryDays`, which sets the client’s review cadence on Today.

- [x] Program builder, templates, preview, publish as version (step 5)
  - **Dependencies:** `@dnd-kit/core` 6.3.1 and `@dnd-kit/sortable` 10.0.0, pinned (both published in December 2024). They load only with the builder and templates screens, in their own chunk.
  - **Model** (`domain/program.ts`, tested): the builder works in weeks of days of prescriptions and turns them into the stored `PlanDay` list and back. Days are counted from the version’s start date (day 0 is the start date), so the builder names days from the date it starts on; templates sit on Monday weeks, and applying one moves the start date to the next Monday. `validateProgram` is the one definition of “can be published”: a name, at least one workout, a main exercise in every workout, one workout a day, every exercise in the library and not archived, 1 to 20 sets, reps that read as `10` or `8-12` (or a duration), sensible loads and rests.
  - **API** (`api/programs.ts`, `api/templates.ts`, Zod-checked, lazy): `getProgramSource` (the current version, any draft, the earliest start: today for a client with no program, tomorrow otherwise, so today’s workout stays as the client expects), `saveProgramDraft` (one draft per client, replaced on each save, never visible to the member: every member read is published-only), `publishProgram` (validated again on the server; must start after the previous version began; the next version number; **never touches an earlier version or any log**; one version per publish key, so a double tap or a retry makes one; audited; removes the draft). Templates: yours and the gym’s shared ones (the owner sees all), saved only as sound programs, edited and archived by their owner (the owner can change any), shared with a switch.
  - **Screens:** `/t/library/programs?member=…` (`features/trainer/Builder.tsx`, `program/*`): the weeks and days (add, copy, remove, rest weeks), the open day with its exercises (sets, reps or range, load, rest, time, a coaching cue; drag by the handle or use the move buttons), “Use a template” (asks before replacing work), “Preview as member” (a day at a time, in the order the client does it, with the targets they log against), “Save draft”, and a publish confirmation that shows what goes live, what changed against the current version and that the earlier version and logs stay, then the new version. Phone, one column and the library as a sheet; from 768 px the weeks and days beside the open day; from 1280 px the library as a third zone, so adding an exercise is one click. `/t/library/templates` (`Templates.tsx`): list, template and actions in three zones, building and editing in the same builder. The library, templates and builder share the Section tabs (Exercises, Templates, Programs). Adding an exercise is announced to screen readers rather than with a toast, which would sit over the work.
  - **Dialogs:** Escape now closes the top-most dialog even when focus has dropped to the page because the focused button was removed when a step finished (found by the builder’s tests).
- [x] Coach-led logging (T05)
  - **The member’s logging, done by the trainer** (`api/coachLog.ts`, Zod-checked, lazy): `getCoachDays` (the client’s scheduled days from two weeks ago to today, how each stands and who entered it), `getCoachWorkout` (the same view the member gets: the prescription, “Best last time”, the pre-fill rules in `domain/workout.ts`), `saveCoachDraft`, `submitCoachWorkout` and `correctWorkout`. It runs on the member’s own code in `api/workout.ts` (`dayFor`, `viewDay`, `saveDraftFor`, `submitFor`), so the draft, the one-log-per-day rule, the idempotency key, badges and “Target reached” notes are the same. Only the assigned trainer (a client who is not theirs is “not found”). A coach may log any past day with a plan but not a day that has not come (`coachLoggingBlockedReason`); a finished log is changed only by a correction. Finishing a draft the member started makes the coach its author.
  - **Entered by:** the log keeps `enteredBy` and `enteredByRole`, and every screen says “Entered by Coach Meera”: the session header, the day list, the client’s Workouts tab, and the member’s workout summary.
  - **Corrections keep the original:** the log shows the latest version and keeps its first author and completion time. Each correction stores what the log said just before it (status, entries, effort, note, who entered it) with the reason, who and when, and writes a `workout.correct` audit event with the reason. A reason is required. The same correction key makes one correction, so a double tap or a retry changes nothing.
  - **Screen** (`features/trainer/Log.tsx`, `/t/log?client=&date=`, reached from the client’s page): client, then day, then the session. The session is the member’s `ExerciseStep` set rows and `useDraftSaver` autosave, one exercise at a time. Phone and 768 to 1279 px: each step is its own screen with a Back link (the session needs the width); from 1280 px the days stay on the left and the session sits beside them. Seed: Arnav Banerjee has four unlogged days, Aisha Agarwal a finished coach log.
- [x] Review and follow-up (T06, P04)
  - **API** (`api/review.ts`, Zod-checked, lazy): `getReview` (adherence and the weekly chart, trends over 12 weeks: body weight, waist and the three lifts done most, each only with two readings or more; the member’s latest check-in and whether it came after your last review; goals with where each stands and “Target reached”; the review that is due and why; the last shared review and its follow-up date; the history of shared reviews and **your own** private notes) and `saveReview` (shared feedback or a private note). A client who is not the caller’s is “not found”.
  - **Shared versus private:** shared feedback is what the client reads (their Today shows the new-review card until they open it) and is audited (`review.shared`); a private note never reaches the client and stays with its author after a transfer. A follow-up date goes only on shared feedback, must be after today and within a year, and becomes the next review date (`reviewDue`); a private note does not move it. One key makes one review.
  - **Confirm achieved:** only goals that show “Target reached” and are still active offer it (`canConfirm`). It runs `confirmGoalAchieved`: only the assigned trainer, the Goal achieved badge is awarded the first time and never again (a second achieved goal for the same client awards none), repeating changes nothing, and each confirmation is audited (`goal.confirm`).
  - **Screen** (`features/trainer/Review.tsx`, `/t/clients/:memberId/review`, from the client’s page): check-in, adherence, trends, goals, the form and the history. Phone: one column. From 768 px: the sections on the left, the review beside them. From 1280 px: the history in a third zone (below that it follows the form).
- [x] Schedule, availability, attended and no-show (O01, O02, T04, M06)
  - **Records** (Dexie v6, seed v11): `TrainerShift` (hours on one date, added to the weekly pattern) and `TrainerLeave` (first and last day, both included; canceling keeps the record). The seed gives Meera a Sunday cover shift at Andheri and a year-end break beyond the booking window, and Farah three days of leave in December.
  - **Rules** (`domain/schedule.ts`, tested; `slots.ts` uses them): leave blocks new bookings on its days, a shift adds windows on its date, overlapping windows are merged so a session is never offered twice; a weekly pattern can’t put a trainer in two places at once; leave starts today or later and runs up to 90 days; a booked session that has not started and falls on a leave day (by the India date) is a conflict. Members’ booking slots (`api/bookings.ts`) read leave and shifts, so a member sees no times on a leave day.
  - **Attended and no-show:** a session is marked by its own trainer, once: “Attended” from its start time, “No-show” from `noShowAfterMinutes` (15) after it starts, and both until `markWindowHours` (48) after it starts; both are `GymPolicies` values the owner edits in M4. After the window the trainer is told to ask the front desk. Attended uses the reserved credit (a `consumption` entry). A no-show uses it or gives it back by `noShowConsumesCredit` (a `consumption` or a `release`). The rule is said before the trainer chooses (“Uses 1 session credit”, “Gives the credit back”), the same mark again changes nothing, and a session marked one way is not changed to the other. Each mark is audited (`booking.attendance`), and the member’s Bookings shows the result and whether the credit was used or returned.
  - **Leave conflicts** (shown to the trainer as agreed): adding leave returns how many booked sessions clash; nothing is canceled. The sessions are listed with “On your leave”, and each can be moved to another free time with the trainer or to another trainer at the same branch (`getConflictOptions`, `moveSession`). The credit stays with the session, the member is emailed (`booking_rescheduled`), the free slot is checked again inside the write, one key moves it once, and the move is audited (`booking.move`). The front desk’s side of the same decision comes in M3.
  - **API** (`api/schedule.ts`, Zod-checked, lazy): `getSchedule`, `saveAvailability`, `addShift`, `removeShift`, `addLeave`, `cancelLeave`, `markAttendance`, `getConflictOptions`, `moveSession`. Branches are limited to the trainer’s own, and changes to availability, shifts and leave are audited (`schedule.change`).
  - **Screen** (`features/trainer/Schedule.tsx`, `/t/schedule?tab=`): Sessions (clashes with leave, sessions to mark, today, the days ahead, earlier this week), Availability (the weekly pattern by day), and Shifts and leave. Phone: tabs in one column. From 768 px: the sections on the left, the chosen one beside them. From 1280 px: a third zone, “Needs you now” (sessions on leave and sessions to mark); below that the same boxes sit at the top of Sessions.
