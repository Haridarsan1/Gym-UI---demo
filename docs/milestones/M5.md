# M5 · Platform admin (R01, S01, S02, S05)

Detailed build notes. The checklist is in [docs/PLAN.md](../PLAN.md#7-milestones); the product requirements are in [docs/REQUIREMENTS.md](../REQUIREMENTS.md).

- [x] **A second demo gym** (seed v27): Lotus Strength Club, Pune, two branches, 42 members, its own staff, plans, invoices and logo (`demo/seedLotus.ts`), on a 14-day trial that ends in 5 days. It is in the role switcher under “Another gym” in the account menu, so tenant isolation can be shown live. Lotus is built the first time someone opens it (signing in as one of its people through the role switcher, or an Ember administrator opening the console), not on a member’s first visit: `ensureLotus()` in `demo/second.ts`, called from `api/context.ts`. Ember’s software invoices are built the same way (`ensureSaasInvoices()`), the first time an owner or an administrator needs them. Unit tests start with one gym; `resetDemo({ secondGym: true })` builds Lotus at once. Pulse Fitness is on the paid Growth plan with four Ember software invoices (EMB/26-27/0001 to 0004, ₹5,308.82 each with GST).
- [x] **Tenants (R01, S01):** `/admin` lists every gym with its subscription state, health (at risk, needs a look, healthy, with the reasons in words), what it uses against each limit (branches, staff accounts, active members) and its set-up progress (account created, owner invited, branches configured, members imported). Filters for at risk and needs a look. **New gym** creates the account on a 14-day trial and sends the owner an invitation through the delivery log. Platform administrators still see no member, money or health records.
- [x] **Tenant detail:** plan, limits, entitlements, billing dates, a state control (with a reason the owner can read), a plan change, plan requests from the owner (approve or decline) and Ember’s software invoices to the gym, kept apart from the invoices the gym raises for its members.
- [x] **Subscription states (S02) enforced in the gym’s app** (`domain/subscription.ts`, `api/context.ts`): trial, paid, past due (a 7-day grace period with a banner for everyone in the gym, then read-only), suspended (read-only; reports and CSV downloads still work), canceled (nothing but a download for 30 days, then nothing). A trial that runs out becomes past due, then suspended. The owner sees each state on Subscription in words, with what it means and a **Pay and reactivate** button; the desk and members see a short banner. Refusals say why in plain words, for example “Pulse Fitness is read-only because its subscription is suspended.”
- [x] **SaaS plans and limits (S02):** `/admin/plans` makes and edits plans (price, limits for branches, staff accounts and active members, feature entitlements) and withdraws one from sale (not one with gyms on it). Limits are enforced where people are added: inviting staff, registering a member, importing members. Reports are a plan feature. The refusal names the plan, the limit and what is in use, and links to **Ask for a larger plan**, which the platform approves or declines.
- [x] **Support access (R01, S05):** `/admin/support`: a request names a gym, a reason (10 characters or more) and 15, 30, 60 or 120 minutes. While it lasts, everyone in that gym (not the other gym) sees a banner with the reason and the end time. Inside, the administrator has the owner’s views, read-only, and no health records; each screen opened writes a “Support looked at a screen” line in the gym’s audit log. The session ends by itself (the administrator is put back in their own console and the owner’s log says so) or on **End support access**. The owner reads started, looked at and ended in **Audit log**, with the reason.
- [x] **A05 acceptance** (`api/a05Acceptance.test.ts`, 12 tests): every record, list, report, export and direct link of one gym is refused to the other, in both directions; a platform administrator has no way in without a session; logos stay with their gym; a progress photo is refused across gyms and members; photo access needs consent and the right role (assigned trainer, an owner or branch manager with the health switch, never desk or finance), and withdrawing consent closes it; exports are role- and branch-scoped, carry no contact details or health records, and are audited. The test found one real leak, which is fixed: the preview of every email template used “Pulse Fitness” as the gym name in the other gym.
- **Backup restore cannot be shown in demo mode.** The A05 requirement “a backup restore in a test environment” is moved to M6B, where there is a real backend to restore.
- **Done when:** support access expires automatically and every action is in the audit log. **Met** (`api/platform.test.ts`, `e2e/platform.spec.ts`, `desktop-flows.spec.ts`).
- **Checkpoint 4** sheets: `docs/checkpoints/m5/{390,768,1440}/` (`E2E_PORT=5199 npm run m5:shots`): the tenants list, a tenant’s detail, a suspended gym as its owner sees it, a support access request, the support banner inside a gym, and the audit entries.

## First-visit size of the member app (after M5)

M5 took `/m` from 157.8 KB to 167.6 KB on a first visit. Lotus and the platform seed were 3.5 KB of that; the rest was in every shell and every API call (banners, the subscription rules, support sessions). What was done, and what is left:

- Lotus and Ember’s invoices are built on first need (`demo/second.ts`, called from `api/context.ts`), not by the first seed.
- `GymBanners` is a small gate; the banners (`GymBannersView`) download only when Ember support has access or the subscription has something to say.
- `domain/subscriptionQuick.ts` answers “paid, or a trial that has not run out” without the wording in `subscriptionStatus.ts`; `api/supportAccess.ts` loads only for a support session or an expired one.
- `bundle-report` now takes every `seed*` chunk off the return-visit figure (it took off only the main seed before, which hid 8 KB of seed code).

| First visit, JS gzip | M4 | M5 as built | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/m` | 157.8 KB | 167.6 KB | 160.4 KB |
| `/t` | 157.3 KB | 165.9 KB | 159.8 KB |
| `/desk` | 157.3 KB | 166.2 KB | 159.9 KB |
| `/owner` | 154.1 KB | 163.1 KB | 158.3 KB |
| `/admin` | 136.3 KB | 146.8 KB | 143.6 KB |

Return visit (no seed code), `/m`: 141.1 KB. The aim of 158 KB for `/m` on a first visit is **not met**: 2.4 KB over. What is left is the seed generator (about 19 KB of the first visit: gym 10.5, training 7.0, plans 1.8) and small M5 additions in the shared chunks (permissions, context, status pills, account list). Closing the gap needs the seed split by who reads it, which changes how the gym’s data is built; it is not done.
